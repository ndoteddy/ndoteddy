## About

**Over a decade of international engineering experience** across **payment, telco, and renewable energy**, designing, building, and operating distributed systems at world scale, in multi-region architectures that demand resilience, low latency, and operational discipline under real production load.

I bring a **Senior Engineer's hands-on depth** paired with an **R&D innovation mindset**, constantly prototyping and pushing new approaches rather than settling for the established pattern. My core stack is **.NET Core and Angular**, extended with **Model Context Protocol (MCP) integration** to connect enterprise applications with AI agents and tooling. On top of that foundation, I architect multi-cloud infrastructure across **GCP, AWS, and Azure**, with deep focus on AI/ML integration and platform engineering.

Currently operating at Principal-level within a global engineering organization, with hands-on ownership of cross-cloud infrastructure and a mandate to deliver systems that are **operationally sound, not just technically impressive**. Outside of work, I'm a hands-on **DIY and tinkerer at heart**, always pulling things apart to understand and rebuild them better.
